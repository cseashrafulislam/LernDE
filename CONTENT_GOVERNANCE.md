# LernDE content governance

## Publication
Draft → Language validation → CEFR/topic mapping → Example validation → Pronunciation/audio check → Reviewed → Published.

## Canonical-data rule
Each German fact has one authoritative record. Lessons, games, review and exams reuse it instead of maintaining conflicting editable copies.

## Lexical provenance
Canonical vocabulary must be a real German lexical item from a reviewed curated record or identified source-backed dataset. Do not publish AI-generated, synthetic or invented lexical entries as course truth. Broad reference vocabulary and CEFR course vocabulary are different concepts.

## Vocabulary review
A mastery lemma should have, where applicable: stable ID, canonical lemma, article/gender/plural, relevant verb forms, contextual Bangla/English meanings, level/topic metadata, checked example, pronunciation support and no duplicate inflection counted as a new lemma.

## Word-tap meaning
Exact match wins. Curated meaning has higher priority than lesson-reference and general-dictionary data. Inflection-to-lemma resolution must be explicit/validated. Do not guess by blindly removing suffixes.

## Pronunciation
Target Standard German. Bangla transliteration is an approximate scaffold, never the authoritative sound source. Device TTS varies; recognition similarity is not a native-accent score.

## Grammar
Rules, constraints and examples must be correct. Mnemonics are memory aids, not replacement rules.

## Exams
Practice tasks are original and must not claim official Goethe/telc authorization.

## Germany-life content
Teach language, not frozen legal/administrative facts. Verify current rules from official sources when they matter.

## AI/translation
Generated assistance is not automatically publishable truth. External provider secrets stay server-side.

## Change discipline
Keep stable IDs where they represent learner progress. Prefer compatible changes and require production validation before release.
