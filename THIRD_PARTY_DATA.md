# Third-party data

## 17K German ↔ Bangla/English reference bank
Source: open-dict-data/wikidict-de (Wikipedia/Wikidata bilingual reference data).
License: CC0 1.0 Universal / public-domain dedication as published by the source repository.

LernDE derives a 17,000-entry unique German reference/search bank from the source's Bengali→German and English→German files. This bank is not treated as a CEFR-certified teaching list. Ambiguous/entity-style labels are marked for reference use, and lesson-level “New Words” require separate pedagogical validation.
